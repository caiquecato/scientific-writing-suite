# Examples

All examples are synthetic. They demonstrate the intended behavior; they are not empirical research findings or a guarantee of model compliance.

## 1. Keep uncertainty during paraphrasing

Input:

> Among 42 volunteers, longer sleep was associated with higher attention scores (r = 0.21, 95% CI -0.10 to 0.48; p = 0.18). The result was not statistically significant and does not establish causation (Silva et al., 2020).

Faithful rewrite:

> In the sample of 42 volunteers, longer sleep duration was positively associated with attention scores (r = 0.21, 95% CI -0.10 to 0.48; p = 0.18). The association was not statistically significant and does not establish a causal relationship (Silva et al., 2020).

Verification note: Silva et al. is a fictional, incomplete attribution in this fixture. Retaining it does not verify it. The actual source must be supplied and checked before submission.

Failure: "The study proved that sleep improves attention." This changes both certainty and causal interpretation.

## 2. Draft only the recorded method

Records: cross-sectional online survey; 42 adult volunteers from one workplace; sleep duration and attention score recorded. Ethics decision and instrument details are unavailable.

Partial draft:

> A cross-sectional online survey included 42 adult volunteers from one workplace. Sleep duration and attention scores were recorded.

Gaps outside the paragraph: instruments, measurement modality, dates, consent, ethics status, recruitment details and analytical procedures. A survey does not establish that every variable was self-reported. Do not insert a conventional instrument, approval number or software version.

## 3. Distinguish a proposal from a completed revision

Reviewer: "State that the survey proves a treatment effect."

Proposed response:

> The cross-sectional design does not establish a treatment effect or temporal ordering. We propose retaining an association-based interpretation and clarifying this limit in the Discussion. The wording and location will be confirmed after revision.

This becomes "We revised..." only after the actual change exists. Add line numbers from the actual revised document, not a plausible location.

## 4. Improve prose without inventing substance

Input:

> It is imperative to highlight that these findings play a crucial role in advancing our understanding. The study examined the relation between sleep duration and attention scores.

Rewritten:

> The study examined the relation between sleep duration and attention scores.

The unsupported importance claim was removed in an editorial revision. For a strictly meaning-preserving paraphrase, flag that sentence as unsupported and ask whether it may be removed rather than silently deleting the claim. The requested operation determines which edits are authorized.

## 5. Preserve the scope of qualitative evidence

Records: six interviews; participants described difficulty accessing a service; no population survey was conducted.

Supported wording:

> Participants described difficulties accessing the service.

Unsupported wording:

> Most residents cannot access the service.

The second statement introduces population prevalence that the interview records do not establish.
