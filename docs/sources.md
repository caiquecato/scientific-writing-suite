# Sources and scope of evidence

Consulted on 11 November 2026. This is a curated exploratory reading list, not a systematic review. Editorial experience, empirical findings and publisher policies are different kinds of evidence. The integrity contract and task output formats are this project's design decisions.

The arXiv utility could not resolve a dependency in the local research environment. Official arXiv pages were consulted through browsing instead. No full papers are bundled.

## Interpretation limits

A verified reference identity does not establish support for a sentence. Citation counts are not readability scores or acceptance probabilities. Results from particular AI detectors or biomedical corpora do not establish the behavior of every current detector or discipline. The suite does not convert these findings into word bans, fixed sentence-length quotas or authorship certification.
